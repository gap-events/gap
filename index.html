Connection begins with a seat at the table.

gặp /gʌp/ — to meet, to choose connection over convenience, to finally sit next to someone more interesting than your phone.

Inspired by the Vietnamese family dinner table, gặp brings expats, travellers and locals together over good food, shared stories and nights worth remembering.

From intimate dinners to big nights out, you bring the appetite. We'll take care of the magic.

Our story

We've never been more connected. Yet somehow, real connection feels harder to find.

We can reach anyone, anywhere, at any time. Technology has made life more convenient, but somewhere along the way, we've traded a little human connection for that convenience.

And yet, some of life's best moments still happen over good food, shared stories and conversations that make us lose track of time.

Inspired by the Vietnamese family dinners we grew up with, we created gặp to bring a little more of that into the world.

No algorithms. No endless scrolling. Just real people, real conversations and a reason to put your phone down.

"The future may be digital. But connection should still be human."
The founders of gặp

The magic bit

A little thing called duyên.

There's a Vietnamese word, duyên, for the invisible connection that brings people into each other's lives.

Call it fate, coincidence or simply being in the right place at the right time. We call it the beginning of something good.

At gặp, we create the space. The rest is duyên.

Meet the two people behind gặp

Two hosts. One shared vision.

United by a shared love of good food and bringing people together, you'll usually find us discovering somewhere new to eat, dreaming up our next gặp event or making sure everyone around us is having a good time.

Huong · more than meets the eye

Life's better in HD.

Huong brings the creative vision, an eye for detail and just the right amount of chaos to gặp. She has a knack for turning a simple dinner into something worth remembering, and strangers into people who feel like old friends. Unapologetically particular about the little things, unfamiliar with the concept of basic and occasionally at odds with the budget, but never one to take herself too seriously.

She believes the best nights aren't just remembered for what was on the table, but for what happened around it.

Tam · a man on a mission

Building something that can never be replaced.

Tam is the one who brings structure to the chaos, turning big ideas into experiences that run seamlessly. He's also the one who notices who hasn't spoken yet and asks the question that turns small talk into a real conversation. Genuinely curious about people, he has a way of making everyone feel like they belong.

He believes the best things in life aren't things at all, but the people we meet and the moments we share along the way.
